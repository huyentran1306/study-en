✨ Platform Features

Everything You Need to
Master English

Six powerful learning modules, each powered by AI to accelerate your English journey.

📊 Dashboard

Your Learning Command Center

The main dashboard gives you a complete overview of your learning journey. Track your daily streaks, monitor progress across all skills, and see personalized AI recommendations at a glance.

  • Visual progress tracker with skill radar chart
  • Daily learning goals and streak counter
  • AI-powered recommendations for next steps
  • Quick access to all learning modules
  • Weekly and monthly performance reports

🎤 Speaking Practice

AI Speaking Coach in Your Pocket

Practice speaking English with an AI coach that listens, analyzes, and gives instant feedback. Powered by Whisper for accurate speech recognition and GPT-4.1 for contextual coaching.

  • Real-time speech-to-text transcription
  • Grammar and fluency analysis
  • Pronunciation scoring (0–100)
  • Natural conversation flow coaching
  • Text-to-speech response with ElevenLabs

💬 AI Chat Assistant

Chat with Your AI English Teacher

An intelligent conversational AI that acts as your personal English tutor. Ask anything, practice writing, explore grammar concepts, or engage in roleplay scenarios.

  • Context-aware conversation memory
  • Soft grammar correction with explanations
  • Multiple AI models (GPT-4.1, Claude, Gemini)
  • Interactive roleplay scenarios
  • Vietnamese translation support

📚 Vocabulary Learning

Smart Vocabulary with Spaced Repetition

Learn vocabulary the scientifically proven way. AI generates contextual example sentences and adapts the review schedule to your memory patterns using spaced repetition.

  • AI-generated example sentences per word
  • Spaced repetition flashcard system
  • Topic-based vocabulary packs (Travel, Business, etc.)
  • Vietnamese translations and explanations
  • Difficulty levels: Beginner → Advanced

🎯 Pronunciation Analysis

Phoneme-Level Pronunciation Feedback

Advanced pronunciation analysis powered by Azure Speech Services. Compare your pronunciation to native speakers and get detailed phoneme-level feedback for rapid improvement.

  • Phoneme-level accuracy scoring
  • Accent detection and analysis
  • Stress and intonation patterns
  • Native speaker comparison
  • Improvement suggestions with examples

  • Multi-dimensional skill tracking
  • Interactive progress charts
  • Weekly and monthly reports
  • AI-powered learning recommendations
  • Strength and weakness identification
