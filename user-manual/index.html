🌟 Overview

What You Can Learn

A complete English learning ecosystem powered by multiple AI models and cutting-edge technologies.

🗣️
Speaking Practice
Practice real conversations with AI. Get instant feedback on fluency, grammar, and natural expression. Role-play real-life scenarios.
💬
AI Chat Assistant
Chat with a professional AI English tutor. Ask grammar questions, practice writing, get contextual explanations and corrections.
📚
Vocabulary Learning
AI-generated example sentences, spaced repetition flashcards, topic-based vocabulary sets with Vietnamese translations.
🎯
Pronunciation Analysis
Advanced speech analysis with Whisper & Azure Speech. Get phoneme-level feedback, accent detection, and fluency scores.
📊
Progress Tracking
Visual analytics dashboard. Track learning streaks, skill improvements, daily goals, and get AI-powered recommendations.
🎭
Roleplay Scenarios
Interactive roleplay scenarios — job interviews, business meetings, travel, daily conversation — with contextual AI feedback.
🚀 Quick Start

Get Started in Minutes

1
Open the App
Launch the Study English app in your browser. Sign in with your account or continue as guest.
2
Choose Your Mode
Select Speaking Practice, AI Chat, Vocabulary, or Pronunciation from the main dashboard.
3
Start Practicing
Begin your session. Speak, type, or listen — the AI adapts to your level and learning pace.
4
Track Progress
Review your analytics dashboard to see improvements, streaks, and AI-powered recommendations.
